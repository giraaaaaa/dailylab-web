FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Today free?

Yes. Today is free on the App Store, including journaling, mood, habit and health logging, and AI replies.

Does AI reply to a one-line entry?

One-liners are always saved. AI replies arrive for entries of about 30 characters or more, with mood tags, a warm comment and a question to think about.

Is my journal used to train AI?

No. Your journal is never used for AI training. AI analysis only runs with your consent and without personal identifiers, and all data is stored encrypted.

Is Today available on Android?

Today is live on the App Store for iPhone. The Android version is in closed beta on Google Play — join the tester group, then install from the testing page.

Which health data does Today read?

With Apple Health (Health Connect on Android) connected, Today reads steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts automatically. Water is logged with a single tap.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time in Settings.
